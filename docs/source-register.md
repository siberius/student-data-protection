## Maintain the record

For a changed legal claim, record exact article/paragraph, jurisdiction, source version, access date, what was read and who reviews the conclusion. A blocked page (403/429), a skipped EUR-Lex URL or an unavailable document is unverified, not passed. The scheduled link job checks reachability; it cannot validate legal meaning. EUR-Lex remains excluded from automated requests because of blocking and requires separate source review. Do not silently turn a Commission summary or a proposal into binding enacted law.

Recheck after an amendment, a changed purpose/provider/jurisdiction or before a legally consequential deployment. Record unresolved conflicts and the responsible follow-up. This register does not settle each school's national/regional retention plan, authority competence, contracts, DPIA/EIPD, FRIA or provider conditions. These are deployment decisions to verify; do not invent compliance while waiting.
